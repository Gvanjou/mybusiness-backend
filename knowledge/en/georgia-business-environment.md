## Can foreigners buy a business in Georgia?
Yes. Georgian law does not require residency to own or buy a business. Company registration is done at the Public Registry (napr.gov.ge) and typically takes about one business day for a modest fee (mybusiness.ge FAQ). Opening a bank account as a non-resident can take longer and banks may ask for extra documents.

## Main tax regimes
- Small business status: individual entrepreneurs with annual turnover up to 500,000 GEL can apply for a 1% tax on turnover (mybusiness.ge FAQ).
- Companies: the standard regime includes 18% VAT (registration becomes mandatory above a turnover threshold) and 15% profit tax, which in Georgia is charged when profit is distributed rather than when it is earned.
- Salaries are subject to personal income tax withheld by the employer.
Tax rules change; confirm the current rates and thresholds with an accountant before buying.

## Licences and permits
Some activities need permits or registrations, for example food businesses (food safety registration), alcohol sales, pharmacies and medical services. When buying, check whether existing permits transfer to the new owner or must be reissued.

## Leases
Many small businesses do not own their premises. The lease is often the most valuable part of a location-based business: check the remaining term, rent increases, and whether the landlord agrees to transfer the lease. A great location with a lease ending in 6 months is a major risk.
