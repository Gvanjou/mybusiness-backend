## Posting a listing (selling a business)
Create an account and post your first listing for free at mybusiness.ge/ka/create: title, price, location, description and photos. Adding real figures (monthly revenue, expenses), the exact location and clear photos makes buyers trust the listing more. All messages from buyers come directly to you; mybusiness.ge takes no commission from the deal.

## VIP, Super VIP and Business packages
Paid packages increase visibility: VIP and Super VIP place a listing higher in the list; the Business package is for owners or agencies selling several properties. Current prices are on mybusiness.ge/ka/packages.

## Searching and filtering
The search page (mybusiness.ge/ka/search) filters by deal type (sale, lease, partnership), category and city. Prices can be shown in GEL or USD.

## Favourites and comparing listings
Logged-in users can save listings to favourites (Dashboard → Favourites). The Compare page (mybusiness.ge/ka/compare) shows 2–3 listings side by side; add listings from their pages with the compare button.

## Contacting a seller
Each listing page shows the seller's contact options (phone and messages). The assistant cannot contact sellers or change listings on your behalf, but it can draft a first message and a list of questions for you.

## Market insights and AI analysis
mybusiness.ge/ka/insights shows market analytics; mybusiness.ge/ka/ai-analysis gives an AI report for a chosen listing (requires login).
